# Demo script

**Who this is for:** whoever presents COSMOS live. Allow about 6 minutes, with two browsers open.

1. **Shared world.** In browser A, open the Worlds tab, copy the **editor** link and open it in browser B. Click **Present** in A and **Follow** in B. B now mirrors A's stop and selection.
2. **K1.** Say: "Build a global solar network, increase its capacity, and show which regions have an energy surplus."
   - Green badges show surplus regions and red badges show deficits.
   - Results shows HVDC exports, transmission loss, unmet demand and achieved K.
   - B updates live, because the result was computed once in SpacetimeDB.
3. **K2.** Say: "Build a Dyson swarm around the Sun, capture 30 percent of its output, and allocate power to a habitat near Mars."
   - A beam runs to Mars, labelled delivered / demand.
   - Follow up with "increase that to 50 percent", then "undo the last change".
4. **K3.** Say: "Expand from our Solar System at 10 percent of light speed, settle each system after 50 years, and build swarms around settled stars."
   - The shared clock starts playing.
   - Scrub the clock bar; B's playhead moves too.
   - Pause, then fork and try 0.01c. Say "compare with the parent".
5. **B2.** Go to Body. Say: "Show this mutation" (HbS), then "apply the reference sequence in this branch".
   - Zoom in to the cell and gene, then back out to the body. O₂ delivery drops and recovers.
   - Open Results → Dependency graph: sequence → protein → haemoglobin → red cell → blood → O₂.
6. **B4.** Go to Room. Say: "Make this window more transparent."
   - Zoom in to the lattice to see fewer reduced (blue) sites and more photons passing.
   - Zoom out to see the room brighten. The lux reading comes from the model.
7. **B6.** Go to Device. Say: "Increase the interaction rate in this device and power these lights."
   - Point out gross vs electrical vs heat. Net power is negative because of the positron supply cost.
   - Count the lamps lit.
8. **Imagine.** Say "imagine this".
   - A job is queued and the worker renders it. The result is labelled illustrative, at this revision.
   - Change the state mid-render to show the result being discarded as stale.
9. **Permissions.** Open the **viewer** link in a private window. Edits are rejected with a reason, and the join codes are hidden.
